Retirement Visa Path

O-A LONG STAY
Age 50+

Official Thai MFA guidance describes O-A for applicants age 50 or older seeking a long stay without working.

  • Apply while outside Thailand through Thai e-Visa in the country of nationality/residence.
  • Passport/document validity requirements apply.
  • Financial evidence: ฿800,000 (≈ $24,100), or ฿65,000/month (≈ $1,955), or permitted combination totaling the required annual benchmark.
  • Criminal-record clearance.
  • Medical certificate addressing prohibited diseases; current MFA guidance says it must be recent.
  • O-A health insurance: coverage of at least ฿3,000,000 (≈ $90,400) for general illness including COVID-19 under the current official guidance.
  • Thai employment is prohibited.
  • 90-day reporting while residing in Thailand.
RETIREMENT EXTENSION
Annual renewal

For continued retirement stay, Immigration requires qualifying financial evidence. Official MFA O-A guidance cites ฿800,000 deposited/transferred in Thailand, ฿65,000 monthly income/pension, or the permitted combination totaling ฿800,000 annually.

Do not move money based only on this page. Before funding the Thai account, confirm the current seasoning, source-of-funds, bank-letter, and extension-document rules with the Immigration office that will process the extension.

O-A document file to build

Spouse: official O-A guidance says an accompanying spouse who is not independently eligible for O-A may be considered for Category O with a marriage certificate. Confirm the exact spouse route with the responsible Thai mission before filing.

Education (ED) Visa Path

The ED category is for study, academic courses, training and other qualifying educational activity. The school/institution is central to the application.

Before applying

1
Select a legitimate eligible school/course.
Confirm the institution can issue the documents required for an ED visa and extensions.
2
Enroll and obtain acceptance.
Official guidance calls for an acceptance/confirmation letter from the program provider.
3
Get school regulatory documents.
Depending on program, this can include school registration and approval from the relevant Thai education authority.
4
Prepare personal evidence.
Passport, photos, financial evidence and any mission-specific documents.
5
Apply through the official e-Visa system when eligible.
Applicants using e-Visa must be outside Thailand when applying.

After approval / arrival

  • ED is commonly single-entry and must be used within the visa validity shown on the approval.
  • Official MFA material describes an initial permission to stay of up to 90 days for ED.
  • Extensions are requested from Thai Immigration and depend on continued eligibility and supporting school documentation.
  • If leaving Thailand while on an extension, check re-entry-permit requirements before departure to avoid losing the permission to stay.
  • Maintain actual attendance and school compliance. Immigration can require evidence supporting genuine study.

ED document file to build

School-specific requirements matter. Before paying tuition, get the school's ED-visa document list, attendance requirement, extension schedule, refund policy, and confirmation that it currently supports foreign-student ED applications.

5

Permission-to-stay date, 90-day reporting where applicable, extension window, school deadlines and re-entry permits.
