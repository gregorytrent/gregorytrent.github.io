Thailand Visa & Remote Work Plan

Family execution plan for the November 15, 2026 move: Gregory retirement path, spouse two-track plan, adult son visitor-to-remote-work plan, and application workflow.
Target Move: Nov 15, 2026
Retirement + Family Strategy
Thailand-Eligible Jobs Only
Updated Sep 13, 2026
Core Strategy
Gregory uses the retirement path based on monthly pension/benefit income. Wife runs two tracks: secure a Thailand-compatible remote role or use the non-working spouse route. Adult son enters as a visitor, does not work on tourist status, searches for a Thailand-compatible remote role, and transitions to an appropriate remote-work visa after qualifying.
Important Financial Correction
The current Destination Thailand Visa checklist asks for financial evidence of at least 500,000 baht. The working plan uses approximately US$15,131 as the September 13, 2026 planning equivalent. The current checklist does not state a mandatory three-consecutive-month holding period. A family bank statement may be accepted with proof of relationship. Verify the live e-Visa checklist again at filing.

Financial method: monthly income. The retirement threshold is 65,000 baht per month; the working September 13 conversion is about US$1,967 per month. Your stated recurring income above US$5,000 per month clears that planning threshold.

3. Adult Son — Lowest-Cost Realistic Plan
Objective: live with family, keep costs low, target roughly US$1,000–US$1,200 per month in earnings, avoid depending on a difficult Thai local-job search, and transition legally into remote work once qualified.

Phase 1 — Before November 15

Apply now for Thailand-compatible remote customer service, customer experience, customer-success associate, account support, and operations-support roles. Use “Thailand allowed” as a hard filter. Prepare passport, birth certificate, résumé, LinkedIn, and digital application materials. If using the tourist-visa route, obtain it before travel.

Phase 2 — First Months in Thailand

Live with family and minimize expenses. Continue interviewing, but do not begin paid remote work while relying only on tourist status. Build or maintain family financial evidence at or above 500,000 baht equivalent and secure a remote contract, employment certificate, or qualifying freelance documentation.

Phase 3 — Remote-Work Visa Application

Apply under the workcation/digital-nomad/remote-worker category from outside Thailand. Use passport, photo, current-location evidence, financial evidence, remote-work proof, and family relationship evidence if using the family bank statement.

4. Application Process — Wife & Son
Weekly target: 15–25 quality applications each. Do not mass-apply to jobs that fail the Thailand test.
1. Verify Thailand eligibility first. If the role is U.S.-only, another-country-only, or requires a language they do not speak, skip it.
2. Tailor the top third of the résumé. Wife: customer success/client leadership. Son: customer support/operations.
3. State the move clearly. Relocating to Thailand in November 2026 and seeking Thailand/APAC-compatible remote work. Do not claim work authorization that has not been obtained.
4. Prepare interview stories. Wife: sales growth, award-winning service, leadership, escalations, retention. Son: assistant-manager duties, scheduling, customer issues, service metrics, reliability, training, and school/time management.
5. Track every application. Company, role, link, date, Thailand eligibility, pay, employment type, interview stage, follow-up date, and visa implications.
6. Follow up in 5–7 business days when a recruiter or contact is available.
7. Do not begin paid work based on assumptions. Confirm the offer/contract and immigration path first.
